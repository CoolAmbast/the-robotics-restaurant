How it started

A family idea that refused to stay small

We opened our doors in 2019 with one belief: great food and great technology don't compete — they belong on the same table. What started as a neighbourhood experiment became a full family destination.

Our robot servers handle the legwork so our kitchen and front-of-house team can focus entirely on the people in the room. Less rushing. More presence. Better dinners.

Fresh, daily preparation — every sauce, stock, and bread made in-house
Family-first environment — designed from day one with kids and grandparents in mind
Technology with warmth — the robots serve, but humans make every connection
Meet the Kitchen

Chef Marco Deluca

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

Marco grew up watching his grandmother cook Sunday lunches for a table of twenty. After a decade in professional kitchens across Italy and Australia, he came home with one goal: to make food that actually felt like home.

He designed every item on the Robotics Restaurant menu to work across generations — dishes kids reach for first and parents linger over longest. The robot servers? His idea too. "More time with guests, less time ferrying plates," he says.
